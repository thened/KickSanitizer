KickSanitizer v–
Made by nedx
Clean chat rebuilds Kick's chat in its own list, so filtered messages leave no gaps. While it is on, the extension keeps a receive-only WebSocket to Kick's chat service to learn about deleted messages and bans — nothing is ever sent, and no data leaves your browser.
Independent extension. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Kick.com.
